# DESIGN RULES — Gully Labs deck (after SlidesCarnival "Personal Branding", id 154102)

Adopted from the template's "Elegant Minimal / Modern Brown" system. These rules are applied on **every** slide; no slide may deviate.

## Palette (3 inks + 1 accent, nothing else)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| bg | `#F4EFE6` | slide background, always |
| ink | `#221B15` | titles, strong text |
| soft | `#4A443C` | body text |
| muted | `#8A8177` | captions, folios, secondary |
| line | `#CFC5B4` | hairlines (0.75 pt), table rules |
| brown | `#8C6248` | the ONLY accent: kickers, numbers, ratings, quotes |

No fills, no chips, no gradients, no shadows. Structure comes from **hairlines + whitespace + type**, never from coloured boxes.

## Type (two families, fixed scale)
| Role | Font | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kicker | Calibri | 10 | UPPERCASE, letterspace +2.5, brown |
| Title | Georgia | 30 | sentence case, ink, max 2 lines |
| Lead | Georgia | 13 | italic, muted |
| Body | Calibri | 11 | soft, line-spacing 1.15 |
| Micro | Calibri | 9 | muted |
| Big number / data | Georgia | 26–40 | brown or ink |
| Folio | Calibri | 9 | letterspace +1.5, muted |

Rules: titles never uppercase; emphasis via *italic Georgia*, never bold colour; data via Georgia numerals; labels via tracked Calibri caps.

## Grid
- Margin 0.9 in both sides; content top 1.9 in; folio baseline 7.1 in.
- One hairline under the title block (full content width).
- Max 2 columns (text 7.6 in + side 3.9 in) with a 0.35 in gutter.
- Photos: thin 1 pt brown frame with a 6 pt offset second frame (template's double-frame motif); caption in Micro beneath; never full-bleed.

## Components
- **Row list**: serif brown number/index → tracked label → body text; separated by hairlines.
- **Tables**: text-only, hairline rules, header row tracked caps (no fill).
- **Matrices (BCG/GE/SWOT)**: hairline axes only; items as *outlined* circles (1 pt brown stroke, no fill) + Micro labels.
- **Bars**: 1 pt brown outline, no fill, value in Georgia beside.
- **Quotes**: Georgia italic, brown, open with a large serif “ .

## Footer (every slide)
Left: `GULLY LABS — CULTURE AS CAPITAL` (Micro, tracked). Right: `NN / 15`.

## Imagery (v3)

center-cropped, with a 1 pt brown offset frame (+0.08 in) and a single 7.6 pt muted caption at y 6.78.
The cover is the exception: full-bleed photo on the right 6.43 in with an inset hairline frame.
